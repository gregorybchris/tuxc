These icons are the Jumbo silhouette from the site header
(src/assets/images/jumbo-head-simplified.png), knocked out in white on
#4B87F7, the blue a route is drawn in on a map or a thumbnail.

The 16px, 32px and 48px tab icons hold the silhouette a little wider in
the frame, since the trunk is the first thing to disappear when the mark
is scaled down. The 180px Apple and 192/512px Android icons are padded
instead, so nothing is clipped when a platform masks the corners.
